Lev Avrohom

Beis Medresh Lev Avrohom is a vibrant and growing kehillah where Bnei Aliyah come together to elevate their Torah and avodah. The kehillah draws inspiration and guidance from Rabbi Yecheskel Weinfeld shlita, a distinguished leader and teacher with decades of experience in Torah and chinuch.

About the shul

The davening is Nusach Ashkenaz with a yeshivah-style atmosphere. In general, the Rov gives a shiur on Friday night between Kabbalas Shabbos and Maariv in Lashon Hakodesh. On Shabbos morning before davening there is a shiur on Tanya in English, and after tefillah the Rov shares his chidushei Torah over Kiddush in English.

Rabbi Weinfeld has authored many sefarim on a wide range of sugyos in Shas, as well as divrei machshava and musar. Explore his sefarim
